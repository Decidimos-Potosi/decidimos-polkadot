# Plan for spending the 190,000 USDT

Polkadot treasury grant to Heitaria Swiss AG for Decidimos. Paid in eight tranches to the Heitaria treasury multisig (TESORO, 2-of-3), each claimable only from its date and only after the delivered milestone is posted in https://forum.polkadot.network/t/18650. Work runs from January to June 2027. One tranche a month, February to September 2027.

| # | Milestone | What the money pays for | USDT | Claimable from |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Payout contracts, payout engine, panel | Batched USDT payouts from the assembly-ratified community rolls, no double payment; panel for Heitaria and CUPCONL to publish, check, veto and freeze | 40,000 | February 2027 |
| 2 | Enrolment desk, assemblies, loading the list | Enrolment desk for residents without a smartphone; ratification of the rolls by the assemblies; rolls of Río Grande and the Nor Lípez communities loaded | 20,000 | March 2027 |
| 3 | Wallet and recovery on mainnet | Wallet hardened for mainnet scale, fees in USDT; recovery with 12 words, family helpers and a trusted contact | 20,000 | April 2027 |
| 4 | Records on Polkadot, public check page | Every payout recorded on Polkadot and checkable by anyone on a public page | 15,000 | May 2027 |
| 5 | Audit and load test | External audit by Hacken: smart contracts US$8,000, secret-ballot cryptography US$14,000; load test with the rural communities and loading the community registers for the 861,292 people of the department (23,000) | 45,000 | June 2027 |
| 6 | Facilitators running the cycle | Facilitators named by the assemblies trained and running enrolment, votes and payouts in their communities | 15,000 | July 2027 |

## Not paid by the treasury

- Fees of the monthly payouts: paid by Heitaria.
- Fees of the residents' own payments: paid by the residents in USDT, deducted from their wallet.
- DOT to activate each new wallet (0.05 DOT each): paid by Heitaria.
- Wallet test by an independent security firm and testing with the communities by Heitaria.
- Long-term maintenance for the life of the plant: funded by the project sponsor.
- The governance platform: already built.
